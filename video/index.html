Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before ordering.

How fast can you deliver my video?▾
Short-form edits deliver in 24 hours. Long-form YouTube edits in 48-72 hours depending on length. Elite Pack orders get priority 48h turnaround.
What footage format do you need?▾
Any common format works — MP4, MOV, or raw camera files. Share via Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer, and I'll handle the rest, including basic stabilization if needed.
How many revisions are included?▾
Starter includes 1 revision, Pro includes 3, and Elite Pack orders also include 3 revisions on the full batch. I keep refining until you're happy with the final cut.
Do you write scripts or just edit?▾
I primarily edit, but I can help structure your footage into a stronger narrative, suggest hooks, and advise on pacing. Full scriptwriting can be added as an extra on request.
Who owns the final video?▾
You do. Once delivered and paid for through Fiverr, the final video and all its assets belong entirely to you, with full rights to use however you like.
