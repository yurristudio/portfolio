Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before ordering.

Do you only build Discord bots? ▾

No — bots can be built for Discord, but just as often they're standalone chat widgets for a website or app with no Discord involved at all. Tell me where you need it to live.

Does the bot or automation run 24/7? ▾

Yes — hosting is set up as part of every package so bots and workflows stay running continuously, not just while your own machine is on.

What tools can automation connect to? ▾

Anything with an API or an n8n integration — CRMs, spreadsheets, forms, email, social platforms, and most common business tools. Tell me what you use and I'll confirm it's supported. No Discord required.

Can the AI use my own data or rules? ▾

Yes — responses can be scoped to your own FAQ, rules, and tone rather than giving generic answers, whether that's a Discord server or a website chatbot.

What's included in ongoing support? ▾

Uptime monitoring and priority bug fixes are included on larger builds. Additional feature requests after delivery can be quoted separately.
